Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Befor account and all associated data at any time.

Fastest way — open the Befor app → Settings → Security → Delete Account. Your account and data are removed immediately.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, email us from the address linked to your Befor account.

We will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days of receiving your request.

Request Account Deletion

Opens your email app with a pre-filled deletion request.

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Your scan history and account data are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and sign-in identity
  • Your scan history and results
  • Your profile and saved settings
  • Your Befor+ plan status on our side — manage or cancel the subscription itself in Google Play or the App Store

The business-identity details we store to detect networks of related fraudulent websites are public information published by those sites — not your personal data. On deletion they are anonymised so they can no longer be linked to you.
